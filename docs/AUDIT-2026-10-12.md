# Catalogue audit · 12 October 2026

All 416 source records received a separate subject/employer search. All 67 named employers also received a recruitment-contact search. Original pages were fetched individually, followed by 149 additional official-page candidates.

The final database distinguishes 217 employer descriptions, 89 secondary descriptions, and 110 records without a usable full description. This is evidence review, not confirmation that 416 internships are open. Some employer candidates redirected to generic vacancy lists and were rejected as subject-level evidence.

95 study-level classifications changed from the previous repository version. Detailed requirements take precedence over listing headers. 14 exact-reference/URL duplicates remain in the historical catalogue but are hidden from the default view. Offers with withdrawn or unavailable links are separated from unresolved access failures.

15 distinct public email addresses are represented across the scoped job contacts and directory. Personal contacts are tied to their published subject; general HR and site contacts carry scope notes. Privacy, invoice, press and fraud-reporting addresses are not application contacts. An old recruitment post establishes publication, not present mailbox activity or responsibility.

Monthly salary figures appear only with source evidence. Unconfirmed amounts are cleared; `0` is the dataset's unknown sentinel and is never displayed as a salary. No annual figure is silently presented as a monthly salary.

The 36 recruitment/research portals were checked for reachability. A failed Renault landing-page URL was replaced with its known Workday recruitment root. Bot-blocked portals remain distinguished from dead URLs. Account creation and authenticated application submission were not tested.

The UI uses Outfit, a restrained layout, a short summary per subject, expandable conditions/contact evidence, favorites and browser-local application tracking. The Google Sheet's machine feed and inventory were updated while preserving its application-status and notes columns.

Checks: four audit-classification regression tests; real-dataset dashboard checks for default M1 filtering, retired-link/M2 exclusion, external contact sources, favorites, tracking, all 416 unique IDs and 36 portals; JavaScript syntax and Git whitespace checks. Browser visual verification was unavailable in this environment.

See `data/audit-2026-10-12.json` for one evidence record per catalogue entry. No applications or emails were sent. Neither technical reachability nor a search-engine result alone is an open-vacancy guarantee.
